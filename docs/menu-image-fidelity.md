# Dish Image Fidelity Standard

Cleo's Munchies uses representative generated photography, but each menu image must closely match the dish name. A coherent visual style never overrides food identity.

## Shared visual system

- Realistic Nigerian/Afro-fusion takeaway food photography
- Warm directional light on a deep charcoal tabletop
- Subtle pepper-red and okra-green accents
- Square, centered, mobile-card-safe composition
- Authentic handmade texture and believable portions
- No people, hands, text, logos, watermarks, unrelated dishes, or malformed utensils

## Category-specific checks

### Breakfast

- Akara and pap: golden bean fritters beside smooth fermented-corn pap.
- Yam and egg sauce: clearly cut boiled yam with tomato, pepper, onion, and egg sauce.
- Beans with yam: stewed beans and identifiable yam pieces.
- Plantain with egg sauce: plantain—not yam—with the egg sauce visible.
- Fritter combinations: plantain fritters must remain distinguishable from whole boiled plantain; pap and bread must appear only where named.

### Indomie

Every image must visibly be a stir-fried instant-noodle dish. Seafood, chicken, beef, sausage and egg, and catfish versions must show their named protein rather than generic noodles.

### Puff puff

Keep recognisable round Nigerian puff-puff forms. Chocolate, spice, sugar, and berry-and-cream versions must differ through their named finish or topping without becoming unrelated doughnuts.

### Appetisers

The named protein must be unmistakable and coated in Nigerian pepper sauce: gizzard, snail, beef, goat, chicken, or prawns.

### Soups

- Seafood okra: green okra body with recognisable seafood.
- Egusi: granular ground-melon-seed body; goat and assorted versions must show different proteins.
- Ogbono: glossy, viscous draw-soup body.
- Oha and afang: dark leafy bodies with distinct leaf texture and assorted meat.
- Efo riro: rich Yoruba spinach stew with red pepper and leafy texture.
- Banga: palm-fruit soup with orange-brown body and assorted meat.
- Plain okra: green chopped-okra body without being presented as the seafood variant.

### Pies

Use recognisable Nigerian hand pies with a cut or opened example revealing the named meat, chicken, or fish filling.

### Shawarma

Show a Nigerian-style toasted wrap with a visible cut face. Chicken, beef, mixed, fish, chips, and sausage variants must include exactly the named filling/accompaniment.

### Moi moi

Show steamed bean pudding, preferably leaf-wrapped or unwrapped with a clean cut face. The named egg, fish, chicken, prawn, or egg-and-prawn filling must be visible.

### Clean grills

Fish dishes must resemble the named whole fish or fillet form. Chicken variants must clearly show plain grilled chicken, potato, or plantain as named. Signature pepper sauce may appear consistently.

### Rice trays

- Jollof: rich tomato-red grains.
- Special fried rice: pale yellow/golden separate grains with diced vegetables.
- Coconut rice: lighter creamy or golden rice with coconut cues, not red jollof.
- White rice: clearly plain white grains.
- Ofada: characteristic locally grown brownish/greenish short grains.
- Native rice: palm-oil-orange local rice with traditional aromatics/protein accents.
- Coconut fried rice: fried-rice vegetable texture plus visible coconut character.
- Smoky party jollof: deeper red, lightly charred/smoky finish distinct from standard jollof.

## Release acceptance

- 61 menu items resolve to 61 unique image URLs.
- Every production image exists, is non-empty, and decodes in Chromium.
- Each category screenshot displays the image directly beside its dish name for visual review.
- Any ambiguous, malformed, text-bearing, or mismatched result is regenerated before deployment.
- Images remain labelled as representative rather than exact portion guarantees.
